Scene: A small coffee shop. Morning. Maya is behind the counter; Leo comes in, out of breath.
Leo: Sorry I'm late. I had missed the bus, so I ran all the way here.
Maya: If you leave home earlier, you won't have to run.
Leo: I know, I know. I used to wake up at six, but then I got used to sleeping in.
Maya: Well, the espresso machine broke down again. It was fixed last week, and now look at it.
Leo: Have you called the technician?
Maya: I have been calling him since seven. He said he would come after lunch.
Leo: If I were the owner, I would buy a new one.
Maya: If you had told me that yesterday, I would have ordered one online.
(A customer walks in.)
Sam: Good morning! Can I get a cappuccino?
Maya: I wish I could make one. The machine is out of order.
Sam: That's too bad. I'm going to try the bakery across the street, then.
Leo: Wait! Let me figure something out. I'll set up the old moka pot.
Sam: You don't have to go to all that trouble.
Leo: It's no trouble. Things are getting more interesting than I expected.
Maya: He's the most stubborn person I've ever met.
